* The app list opens the instant you touch the edge, instead of fading in.
* Tapping a row launches it — a tap beside a label no longer closes the list.
* Rows show a press highlight, and follow the right-aligned layout setting.
* Both ends of the list stretch and settle the same way, and either can be pulled to close the list.
* Returning to the home screen fades in rather than cutting.
* Launching an app no longer flashes the home screen first.
* The Now Playing card opens whatever is playing when tapped, and no longer draws its own background.
* Album art keeps its rounded corners and fills its square instead of letterboxing.
